This project presents the design and development of a smart meter for 
measuring the mass flow rate of air using three types of sensors.  
The primary goal of this system is to accurately monitor and measure air flow 
in various engineering applications. 
The smart meter employs a combination of advanced sensor technologies to 
capture data on air velocity and density, which is then used to calculate the 
mass flow rate. The system integrates these sensors with a microcontroller 
and software to process the measurements and provide real-time, digital 
readings. This design ensures high precision, reliability, and ease of use, 
making it suitable for a wide range of applications. 
The smart meter features a user-friendly interface and is designed to provide 
instant digital readouts, making it accessible for both professional and general 
use. 
The proposed solution also considers practical aspects such as cost
efficiency, ease of installation, and minimal maintenance, making it an ideal 
choice for both small-scale and large-scale applications. This report details 
the development process, technical design choices, sensor selection, and the 
performance evaluation of the system.
